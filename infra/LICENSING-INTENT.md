# Licensing Intent

## Current Status

`infra/` is currently part of a proprietary monorepo owned by **Impilo Technologies Private Limited (a State Owned Enterprise), registered in the Republic of Zimbabwe**.

This tree contains platform infrastructure, gateway, identity, runtime, and deployment-sensitive material.

## Intended Future Direction

Infrastructure and deployment internals are expected to remain proprietary by default. Public documentation may be published separately later, but not by implying a licence grant over these operational assets.

## Important Boundary

No licence grant exists unless and until a specific artefact is explicitly extracted or relicensed.
