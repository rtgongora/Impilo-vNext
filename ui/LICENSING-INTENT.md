# Licensing Intent

## Current Status

`ui/` is currently part of a proprietary monorepo owned by **Impilo Technologies Private Limited (a State Owned Enterprise), registered in the Republic of Zimbabwe**.

This tree includes product applications, operational consoles, platform shells, and sensitive workflow surfaces.

## Intended Future Direction

The default direction is continued proprietary control. Public-facing demos, samples, or isolated reference artefacts may later be separated if they can be published without exposing sensitive workflow or control-plane logic.

## Important Boundary

No licence grant exists unless and until a UI artefact is explicitly extracted or relicensed.
