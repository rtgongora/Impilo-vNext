# Licensing Intent

## Current Status

`libs/` is currently part of a proprietary monorepo owned by **Impilo Technologies Private Limited (a State Owned Enterprise), registered in the Republic of Zimbabwe**.

The presence of reusable libraries in this tree does not create an open-source licence grant.

## Intended Future Direction

Selected external-facing SDKs or connector libraries may later be extracted and separately licensed, potentially under Apache 2.0, where they are safe for ecosystem reuse.

Examples that may be reviewed later include connector and SDK-style libraries. Internal policy, observability, security, and control-plane libraries are expected to remain closed by default.

## Important Boundary

No licence grant exists unless and until a library is explicitly extracted or relicensed.
