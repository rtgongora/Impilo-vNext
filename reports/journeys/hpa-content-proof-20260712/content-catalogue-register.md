## REVIEW_REQUIRED items (each with its exact substantive reason)

1. **AEU-028** (p46): The manual lists two conflicting minimums under the same bullet: '2 x switched socket outlets per bed, minimum' and '1 x switched socket outlet per bed minimum'. It is unclear whether the minimum is 1 or 2 per bed, or whether the two lines refer to different outlet types.
2. **AEU-033** (p46): The manual lists two near-identical sub-bullets under 'Wash hand basin': '1 per ward, with handless taps, hot and cold running water' and '1 per ward, with hot and cold running water'. It is unclear whether two separate basins are required (one with handless taps, one without) or whether this is a duplicated line.
3. **AEU-040** (p46): The manual lists 'Piped light' as an equipment item, which is unclear as written (it sits between 'Procedure light' and 'Piped oxygen flow meter device' and may be a typographical artefact).
4. **AAM-005** (p70): Duplicate item number in the manual: page 70 numbers both 'Does the service have a quality assurance program in place?' and this item as '1-04', then continues at 1-05. Both items are preserved with the numbering as printed.
5. **ALL-011** (p16): Wording is internally incoherent: 'Floors for executive clinics which attend to executives must have some comfort such as sofas' — a floor cannot 'have sofas'; likely intends comfortable seating (sofas) in executive clinics, but the manual's wording is preserved unresolved.
6. **ALL-023** (p17): Internal inconsistency: 'should be displayed' (recommendation wording) is coupled with 'a non-compliance fee shall be charged for failure to comply with this requirement' (mandatory enforcement); the General section also states display as mandatory under Section 106 (i) (see ALL-003).
7. **CLN-022** (p50): The manual states 'Hydrocortisone x vials' without specifying the number of vials, unlike neighbouring entries ('50% Dextrose x 2 vials', 'Adrenaline x 2 vials').
8. **DEN-001** (p22): Manual states 'Room size 4 x 4' with no unit of measure given (presumably metres, but the manual does not say).
9. **DEN-014** (p22): Manual states 'Hydrocortisone x vials' without a number of vials (other tray entries state 'x 2 vials').
10. **MAM-004** (p35): The manual states omission is permitted 'if approved by FANR'; elsewhere in the same imaging chapter (page 31) shielding compliance is referenced to RPAZ. The approving authority named here appears inconsistent with the rest of the manual.
11. **MAT-029** (p27): The manual states 'Hydrocortisone x vials' — the number of vials is missing (compare '50% Dextrose x 2 vials', 'Adrenaline x 2 vials'); the required quantity cannot be determined from the source text.
12. **MAT-124** (p30): 'Sluice room' appears as the final bullet under the Postnatal Ward 'Equipment' list, but it is a room/facility rather than equipment; the manual's placement is ambiguous as to whether it belongs to the Postnatal Ward equipment schedule or is a separate facility requirement.
13. **MAT-128** (p30): The manual phrases this prohibition with 'should not' ('PCNs and nurse aides should not do deliveries'), which under the obligation-word convention maps to RECOMMENDED, yet the content reads as a prohibition; the intended obligation strength (recommended practice vs mandatory prohibition) is unclear from the source wording.
14. **MCR-017** (p20): Manual reads 'Hydrocortisone x vials' — the number of vials is not stated (compare '50% Dextrose x 2 vials'); required quantity is unclear.
15. **MCR-029** (p21): The equipment list under MEDICAL consulting rooms includes optometry/ophthalmic-specific instruments ('Trial frame and trial set/phoroptor', 'Slit lamp', 'Vertometer/lensmeter', 'Tonometer', 'Retinoscope and ophthalmoscope') that appear misplaced for a general medical consulting room; the manual states them here nonetheless, and the list closes with 'Other diagnostic equipment suitable for the practice'.
16. **OPT-003** (p24): Manual bullet reads only 'Ceiling' with no stated attribute or standard (other sections specify finishes; here nothing is specified).
17. **PHM-025** (p24): Manual wording 'Appropriate prescription temperature recording system' is unclear — it appears to conflate temperature recording with prescription recording, and a separate bullet 'Appropriate prescription recording system' also exists in the same list.
18. **THR-078** (p56): Manual text reads 'Room to be adjustment to operating suite' (page 56) — 'adjustment' is presumed a typographical error for 'adjacent', but the operative wording is garbled.
19. **THR-090** (p58): Manual wording is garbled: 'These are pulse oximetry, conscious electrocardiograph pulse, automated interval blood pressure monitor and temperature, amongst others' (page 58) — the exact intended monitor list ('conscious electrocardiograph pulse') is unclear.
20. **THR-099** (p59): Near-duplicate of the earlier bullet on the same page: 'No fewer than two staff (of whom at least one must be a registered practitioner) should be present...' vs 'At least two appropriately trained staff should be present...' — the two statements differ on the required qualification (registered practitioner vs appropriately trained) and the manual does not reconcile them.
