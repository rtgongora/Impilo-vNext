# Licensing Intent

## Current Status

`services/` is currently part of a proprietary monorepo owned by **Impilo Technologies Private Limited (a State Owned Enterprise), registered in the Republic of Zimbabwe**.

This tree contains the strategic service estate of the platform and is closed by default.

## Intended Future Direction

The default direction for service implementations is continued proprietary control. If partner-safe adapters or public-facing interface artefacts are later separated, they should be extracted as distinct artefacts rather than relicensing this service tree wholesale.

## Important Boundary

No licence grant exists unless and until a specific artefact is explicitly extracted or relicensed.
