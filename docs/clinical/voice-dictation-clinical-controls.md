# Voice dictation — clinical controls

## Clinical safety

1. **Dictation output is unverified clinical content** until the clinician **reviews and saves** (or explicitly discards).
2. **Structured orders, doses, allergies, and coded diagnoses** must not be inferred solely from dictation without **confirmed** structured entry.
3. **High-risk workflows** (prescribing, lethal dose, controlled substances) may **disable** dictation on specific fields by product policy — document per-field in the coverage matrix.

## Documentation parity

Dictated narrative should appear in the same **legal record** category as typed narrative for the same field (subject to local regulation).

## Training

Operators should be trained that **accent, noise, and homophones** cause errors — review is mandatory.
