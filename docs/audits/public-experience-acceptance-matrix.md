## Deferred capability statement

Completion of the public experience does not re-label missing backend contracts as live.
Guest Khuluma chat, temporary emergency identity, SMS/USSD/voice, product-registry BFF
browse, guest basket, anonymous cost estimate and public payer-network search remain
explicitly deferred in the capability register. Their visible alternatives are real
public status, guidance, discovery or sign-in paths.
