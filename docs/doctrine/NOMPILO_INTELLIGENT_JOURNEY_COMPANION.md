# Nompilo Intelligent Journey Companion

Nompilo is Impilo vNext's intelligent journey companion across person, provider, and platform journeys.

## Role

- explain the current step and next allowed action;
- provide accessibility-aware guidance;
- surface next best actions and safe recovery options;
- collect feedback through omnichannel channels;
- support human handoff when needed.

Nompilo is not:

- a replacement for clinical judgement;
- a hidden decision engine for consent, payment, or access;
- a separate source of registry/clinical/financial truth.

## Companion Modes

- PASSIVE_AVAILABLE
- CONTEXTUAL_COMPANION
- STEP_BY_STEP_GUIDE
- ACCESSIBILITY_SUPPORT
- FEEDBACK_CAPTURE
- HUMAN_HANDOFF
- OPERATIONS_INSIGHT

## Accessibility and Feedback

Nompilo should support simple language, read-aloud, keyboard navigation, high contrast, reduced motion, low-bandwidth guidance, and proxy support where relevant.

Feedback channels include in-app, web portal, SMS, WhatsApp-style messaging, USSD, kiosk, call centre, email, CHW-assisted capture, and facility feedback desk.
