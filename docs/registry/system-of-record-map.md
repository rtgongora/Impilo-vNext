| `pct-service` | clinical | **The person Care Continuum (G-CC1):** pct-service owns the cradle-to-grave clinical journey — every care-path service (inpatient, theatre, trauma, ED, paediatric, booking, referral, telemonitoring, community) is a subordinate component; simba-service is the only peer (Wellness Continuum); BUTANO is the record-authority the continuum projects into, not a component; daidzai's trauma spine is correlation *delegated* by the continuum; every clinical record must carry a resolvable PCT anchor (journey_id / encounter_ref / admission handshake) — see [care-continuum-doctrine](../doctrine/care-continuum-doctrine.md). Must not own the contraceptive eligibility rules (WHO MEC/SPR), the antenatal or postnatal contact schedule, or the labour-monitoring standard — all four are governed content in clinical-knowledge-platform-service, so a change of national policy is a content release rather than a code deploy. **Must never issue an identity for a fetus:** identity begins at the birth record, VITO mints it, and nothing before birth may hold one. |
